QUADRICEPS EXERCISES

Exercises for stronger front leg muscles.

Barbell Squat

A compound exercise that strongly trains the legs.

Technique:

  • Place the bar on your shoulders.
  • Squat down with control.
  • Stand up through your feet.

Leg Press

Machine exercise focused on the quadriceps.

Technique:

  • Place your feet on the platform.
  • Lower the platform slowly.
  • Push it back upward.

Leg Extension

Isolates the quadriceps using a machine.

Technique:

  • Sit comfortably on the machine.
  • Extend your legs forward.
  • Lower them slowly.
